SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. Scope of Services
Defines deliverables, milestones, SLAs, and acceptance criteria.

2. Payment Terms
Specifies fees, invoicing schedule, taxes, and late payment penalties.

3. Confidentiality
Obligates protection of non-public information and data.

4. Term & Termination
Start date, renewal, termination for cause/convenience, notice periods.

5. Liability
Limitations, exclusions, and indemnification basics.

Use when drafting primary services contracts.
